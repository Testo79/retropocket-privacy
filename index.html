PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 24 November 2026

RetroPocket ("the app") is a retro handheld emulator. This policy explains what data the app handles.


1. YOUR GAMES AND SAVES STAY ON YOUR DEVICE
Game files you import, save files, save states, screenshots, settings and control layouts are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded by the app. RetroPocket has no user accounts.


2. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)
The app shows optional rewarded video ads only when you choose "Watch ad" to unlock a skin. Ads are never shown during gameplay. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:
- the device advertising ID,
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data.
In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Setup > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites


3. PURCHASES (GOOGLE PLAY BILLING)
Skin purchases are processed by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation of which skins you own, so it can unlock them.
Google Play's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy


4. BOX ART DOWNLOADS
When you ask the app to download box art, it requests images from thumbnails.libretro.com using the game's title. No other data is sent. This feature is used only when you request it.


5. NO ANALYTICS, NO SELLING OF DATA
The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK of its own and does not sell personal data.


6. CHILDREN
The app is not directed at children under 13.


7. DATA DELETION
Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your device. Purchases remain linked to your Google account and can be restored by reinstalling.


8. CONTACT
For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address shown on the app's Google Play store page.
